# Display modes: Normal, Light, Advanced

*[Leia em português](pt-BR/display-modes.md)*

Three modes that control how much of Deck Shelves you see and use — not
different apps, just different amounts of the same settings surface
exposed. Switch anytime from settings, under Additional features.

## Normal (default)

Everything's available at whatever you've set it to. All the settings tabs
are there (minus Advanced), and every decoration/toggle works as
configured.

## Light — "just give me shelves"

A stripped-down experience if you don't want to tinker — good for someone
who just wants clean shelves on Home, or as a quick "demo mode" when
showing the plugin to someone new.

What it does:
- Turns off per-shelf logo/icon/description/hero art on Home, and disables
  Quick Search and Side Nav — regardless of what those toggles are set to
  underneath.
- Hides the controls for all of that in settings, so there aren't a bunch
  of dead switches sitting around doing nothing.
- Trims settings down to just Shelves, Profiles, and Backup.
- Simplifies the shelf editor (no multi-source composites, no manual
  drag-sort, no multi-key sort) and the template picker (simpler templates
  only).

**Nothing is deleted or reset.** Every hidden toggle keeps its saved value
and comes right back — decorations included — the moment you turn Light
mode back off.

## Advanced — "power user"

Everything Normal has, plus a dedicated **Advanced** tab: verbose logging,
an on-device diagnostic log viewer (with copy/clear), and quick reset
shortcuts (reset just shelves, just smart shelves, everything, or specific
categories). These tools are only reachable in Advanced mode — nowhere
else exposes them, on purpose.

## Switching

Light and Advanced can't both be on — turning one on while the other's
active asks you to confirm and switches the other off. Both off = Normal.
The mode is saved per-profile, so different profiles can run in different
modes if you want that.
